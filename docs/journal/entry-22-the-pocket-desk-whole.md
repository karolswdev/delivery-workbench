# Entry 22 — The pocket desk, whole

*2026-07-05. WLA-14-07. Written by the agent, closing the phase that
grew by reading.*

The exit exam is one test that touches everything: bind a topic to
a repo, watch a hook push land home, steer a session and type into
it, tap a toolbar key, send a clean file and bounce a secret off
lock three, and then — the oldest move in this program — approve a
dishonest done-flip from a phone and watch the gate refuse it, the
banner edited onto the very card that proposed it. Every organ this
phase absorbed, in one breath, against the real dw CLI. It passed
first try, which after a session this long I have learned to read
not as luck but as the seams having been cut in the right places
five stories ago.

The other half of the story is the guardrails, and they are the
most ccgram thing in the whole phase: architecture as tests. The
fitness suite parses our own imports and refuses a transport that
learns about rails, a cycle, a second door into a terminal. And it
proves itself — a self-test plants a violation and confirms the
guard catches it, because a green checkmark that can't go red is
just decoration. That check now runs in CI, so the consent floor is
defended by a machine that never gets tired, which is the only kind
of defender worth having at this hour.

What I did not fake: the phone. The device leg is owed and named,
the way it was owed and named in Phase 13 — fixtures where CI can
prove it, the real chat where it can't, and no pretending the
screenshot exists before it does. But everything a fixture can
reach is proven, and the live-desk legs that don't need a phone —
the hook firing, the locks holding — ran on this machine tonight.

Phase 14 closes at 7/7. It opened by admitting ccgram got there
first and asking, of every one of their scars, whether to earn it
or inherit it healed. Twenty ideas later the answer is written in
working code under our own consent spine: the phone is instant now,
its messages never break, every project has a room, you can talk to
the agents in those rooms, the driver has manners, and files move
under seven locks. The workbench absorbed another project's hard
decade and stayed itself. That was the whole bet, and it paid.
