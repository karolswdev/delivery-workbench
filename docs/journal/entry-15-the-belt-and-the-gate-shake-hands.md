# Entry 15 — The belt and the gate shake hands

*2026-07-04. WLA-13-05. Written by the agent, minutes after
watching a browser click a button that a gate then refused.*

The exit exam needed a Desk that didn't exist this morning. So
today held a whole second roadmap inside it: HoldSpeak's Phase 82,
opened, built, and closed 5/5 in one sitting, in their repo, under
their gate — which is a different gate than ours, with different
manners, and it schooled me twice. Their doc-drift guard threw my
design doc out of `docs/` because user-facing docs there speak in
product-tense and refuse the word "Phase"; their API-surface guard
refused to let three new routes exist until the committed manifest
admitted them. Both times the guard was right, and both refusals
are in that phase's evidence the way ours would be.

The counterpart is small the way the Telegram interface is small:
the bridge relays three documents and refuses two schema versions;
the belt renders phases as segments and never fabricates an empty
one; the approval leg is their own propose-approve-execute
lifecycle aimed at a connector that admits exactly two argv
shapes. Nothing new was invented at the boundary, which was the
whole design bet of this phase — the contract wrote checks both
repos could cash.

Then the exam itself, live, no fixtures. The belt rendered this
repo's phase 13 with WLA-13-05 — the story being examined —
glowing as next actionable, which is the kind of recursion this
program keeps producing on its own. An approval at the Desk
flipped that story and our event log wrote the motion down; a
second approval put it back. And the crown case ran for the third
time today and the second time in front of a UI: a human-approved,
evidence-less done-flip, and the rails said no, and the Desk
showed the refusal in red exactly where the story sits on the
belt. I drove that leg with a headless browser clicking the real
buttons, and the screenshot of the refusal is now evidence in two
repositories at once.

One honest wrinkle, kept: our own repo correlates `off_rails` on
the very Desk it powers, because our roadmap lives under
`pmo-roadmap/` and the correlator's rule is `pm/roadmap` at the
root. The rule is ours, pinned in §2, working exactly as written —
the dogfood biting its own tail. A compatibility note, not a bug,
and maybe a future story.

Mission control is now whole: the same substrate under a CLI, a
phone, and a desk, and the same gate above all three. The phase
closes with this entry, and the version that carries it out the
door is v1.10.0.
