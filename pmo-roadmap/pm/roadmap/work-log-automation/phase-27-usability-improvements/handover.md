next-step preview, setup comparison, Workbench, and installation remain usable
without creating program policy, a run/program store, permission, a process,
polling, or work.

work, assign responsibility, judge review, calculate new authority, decide
recovery, or apply an action. Existing exact preview/apply tokens, finite
grants, independent assignment, event/receipt replay, stale-state refusal, and
